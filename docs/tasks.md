# Tasks

Notes on what the adventures ask of the agent. How results are measured is not decided yet.

## The end goal

- Each story has 3–5 plots.
- The plots are fairly complicated: completing them takes 30–240 steps — for a human player, anything from 15 minutes to 2 hours.
- The plots overlap with each other on some salient concepts, but not on all of them.
- The plots are graded from easy to hard.

## Easy and hard plots

An **easy** plot is fairly obvious. In a romantic story, an easy plot is the way the male protagonist finds to get closer to the woman of his dreams.

A **hard** plot is very unobvious. In a romantic story, it can be a deeper meaning: the effects of class inequality in society.

## A plot is a trajectory of events

Each plot consists of a trajectory of events, and that trajectory is not necessarily linear.

- An easy plot probably has no internal branching and is fairly linear.
- A hard plot has multiple branches and jumps across time.
